# Past Perfect 복습 워크북

> [!tip] 빠른 복습용
> 핵심만 빠르게 훑어보고 자가 테스트로 마무리하세요.

---

## ⚡ 핵심 공식

### 기본 구조
```
had + 과거분사(p.p.) = 과거의 과거 (더 먼저 일어난 일)
```

### 한 줄 요약
> **"과거 시점보다 더 이전에 완료된 동작/상태를 표현"**

### 신호 단어
| 과거완료와 자주 쓰이는 표현 |
|---------------------------|
| by the time, before, after, already, just, never, ever, until, once, when |

### 공식 적용
```
When I arrived, the train had left.
     [과거]              [과거완료: 더 이전]
```

---

## ⚠️ 한국인 오류 TOP 5

| # | ❌ 틀린 표현 | ✅ 올바른 표현 |
|---|-------------|---------------|
| 1 | I had visited Paris last year. | I **visited** Paris last year. |
| 2 | When I had arrived, they left. | When I **arrived**, they **had left**. |
| 3 | He had went to school. | He **had gone** to school. |
| 4 | I have finished before he came. | I **had finished** before he came. |
| 5 | I had been waking up at 6 for years. | I **had been waking** up... (진행) 또는 **had woken** up... |

### 핵심 포인트
- 단일 과거 사건에는 과거완료 ❌
- 먼저 일어난 동작에 had 붙이기
- 불규칙 과거분사 주의 (went → gone)
- 기준점이 과거면 과거완료, 현재면 현재완료

## 🔑 핵심 예문 12선

### 초급 (4개)
| 예문 | 해석 |
|------|------|
| I'd eaten lunch. | 나는 점심을 먹은 상태였다. |
| The bus had left. | 버스가 이미 떠난 후였다. |
| She hadn't called me. | 그녀는 나에게 전화하지 않았었다. |
| Had you seen it before? | 전에 그거 본 적 있었어? |

### 중급 (4개)
| 예문 | 해석 |
|------|------|
| By the time I got home, my roommate had already cooked dinner. | 집에 도착했을 때 룸메이트가 이미 저녁을 해놓았다. |
| She realized she'd left her wallet at the cafe. | 지갑을 카페에 두고 온 걸 깨달았다. |
| They'd been friends for years before they started dating. | 사귀기 전에 수년간 친구였다. |
| The concert had sold out by the time we tried to buy tickets. | 표를 사려고 했을 때 이미 매진이었다. |

### 고급 (4개)
| 예문 | 해석 |
|------|------|
| Had the government not intervened, the financial system would have collapsed. | 정부가 개입하지 않았다면 금융 시스템이 붕괴했을 것이다. |
| By the time the truth emerged, the damage had already been done. | 진실이 드러났을 때 피해는 이미 발생한 후였다. |
| The investigation uncovered that the corporation had evaded taxes for decades. | 수사 결과 수십 년간 탈세해 왔다는 것이 밝혀졌다. |
| I'd been contemplating a career change for months before I resigned. | 사직하기 몇 달 전부터 이직을 고민하고 있었다. |

## ⚡ 빠른 자가 테스트 (10문제)

### 빈칸 채우기 (3문제)

**1.** By the time the police arrived, the thief _______ (escape).
> [!success]- 정답
> **had escaped**

**2.** I _______ never _______ (taste) mango before my trip to Thailand.
> [!success]- 정답
> **had ... tasted**

**3.** She was upset because her boyfriend _______ (forget) their anniversary.
> [!success]- 정답
> **had forgotten**

### 오류 교정 (2문제)

**4.** ❌ When I had finished my work, I went home.
> [!success]- 정답
> ✅ When I **finished** my work, I went home. (또는: After I **had finished** my work, I went home.)
> 💡 When절이 먼저 일어난 동작이면 when절은 과거단순, 주절이 그 다음

**5.** ❌ He said he has completed the assignment.
> [!success]- 정답
> ✅ He said he **had completed** the assignment.
> 💡 간접화법에서 시제 일치

### Fill the Gap (3문제)

**6.** 내가 공항에 도착했을 때, 비행기는 이미 이륙한 후였고 다음 항공편을 기다려야 했다.
> [!success]- 정답
> By the time I arrived at the airport, the plane had already taken off, and I had to wait for the next flight.

**7.** 그녀가 승진하기까지, 그녀는 10년간 같은 부서에서 일한 경험이 있었다.
> [!success]- 정답
> By the time she got promoted, she had worked in the same department for ten years.

**8.** 만약 그가 경고를 무시하지 않았더라면, 사고를 피할 수 있었을 것이다.
> [!success]- 정답
> Had he not ignored the warning, he could have avoided the accident. (또는: If he hadn't ignored the warning, he could have avoided the accident.)

### 어순 배열 (2문제)

**9.** [ had / she / the / before / read / book / movie / watching / the ]
> [!success]- 정답
> **She had read the book before watching the movie.**

**10.** [ time / by / arrived / the / we / , / had / the / restaurant / closed ]
> [!success]- 정답
> **By the time we arrived, the restaurant had closed.**
