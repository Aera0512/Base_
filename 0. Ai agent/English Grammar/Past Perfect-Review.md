## Quick Review: 핵심 개념

### 1분 복습
- **Past Perfect = had + 과거분사(p.p.)**
- **역할**: 과거의 과거 (더 이전에 완료된 동작)
- **핵심 신호**: by the time, before, after, already, just, never

### 공식 체크
| 형태 | 구조 | 예시 |
|------|------|------|
| 긍정 | had + p.p. | I **had finished** |
| 부정 | hadn't + p.p. | She **hadn't seen** |
| 의문 | Had + S + p.p.? | **Had** you **eaten**? |

## Section A: 빈칸 채우기 (10문제)

**1.** By the time I arrived, the meeting _______ already _______ (start).

**2.** She was upset because she _______ (lose) her keys.

**3.** _______ you ever _______ (visit) Japan before that trip?

**4.** He couldn't pay because he _______ (forget) his wallet.

**5.** The movie _______ (begin) by the time we got to the theater.

**6.** I _______ never _______ (see) such a beautiful sunset before.

**7.** They _______ (live) in Seoul for 10 years before moving to Busan.

**8.** She told me that she _______ already _______ (finish) the report.

**9.** _______ the train _______ (leave) before you arrived at the station?

**10.** I didn't recognize him because he _______ (change) so much.

> [!success]- 정답 확인
> 1. had ... started
> 2. had lost
> 3. Had ... visited
> 4. had forgotten
> 5. had begun
> 6. had ... seen
> 7. had lived / had been living
> 8. had ... finished
> 9. Had ... left
> 10. had changed

## Section B: 오류 교정 (5문제)

각 문장의 오류를 찾아 수정하세요.

**1.** I have finished my homework before my mom came home.

**2.** When I had arrived, everyone left.

**3.** She had went to the store already.

**4.** By the time we arrived, the concert already started.

**5.** He said he finished the project yesterday.

> [!success]- 정답 확인
> 1. I **had finished** my homework before my mom came home.
> 2. When I **arrived**, everyone **had left**.
> 3. She **had gone** to the store already.
> 4. By the time we arrived, the concert **had already started**.
> 5. He said he **had finished** the project **the day before**.

## Section C: 문장 변환 (5문제)

주어진 두 문장을 과거완료를 사용해 하나로 결합하세요.

**1.** I ate lunch. Then I went out.
→ _______________________________________

**2.** The movie started. We arrived at the theater.
→ By the time _______________________________________

**3.** She finished her work. Then she left the office.
→ After _______________________________________

**4.** I never tried sushi. Then I went to Japan.
→ I _______________________________________ before I went to Japan.

**5.** He lost his phone. He couldn't call me.
→ He couldn't call me because _______________________________________

> [!success]- 정답 확인
> 1. I had eaten lunch before I went out. / After I had eaten lunch, I went out.
> 2. By the time we arrived at the theater, the movie had already started.
> 3. After she had finished her work, she left the office.
> 4. I had never tried sushi before I went to Japan.
> 5. He couldn't call me because he had lost his phone.

## Section D: 한영 번역 (5문제)

**1.** 내가 역에 도착했을 때, 막차는 이미 떠난 후였다.

**2.** 그녀는 파리에 가본 적이 없었다.

**3.** 우리가 도착하기 전에 비가 그쳤다.

**4.** 그는 그 영화를 전에 본 적이 있다고 말했다.

**5.** 만약 내가 알았더라면, 그 계약서에 서명하지 않았을 것이다.

> [!success]- 정답 확인
> 1. By the time I arrived at the station, the last train had already left.
> 2. She had never been to Paris.
> 3. The rain had stopped before we arrived.
> 4. He said that he had seen the movie before.
> 5. Had I known, I wouldn't have signed the contract. / If I had known, I wouldn't have signed the contract.

## Section E: 실전 스피킹 연습

아래 상황에 맞게 과거완료를 사용해 문장을 만들어 보세요.

**1.** 친구와 약속 장소에 도착했는데 친구가 이미 떠난 상황
→ _______________________________________

**2.** 시험 결과가 좋지 않았던 이유 (공부를 안 해서)
→ _______________________________________

**3.** 그 식당에 처음 가본 경험
→ _______________________________________

> [!success]- 예시 답안
> 1. When I arrived at the meeting place, my friend had already left.
> 2. My test results were bad because I hadn't studied.
> 3. I had never been to that restaurant before last weekend.
