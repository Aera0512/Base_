## 에세이 구조 (300 Words)

### 1. Introduction (서론) ~60 words

**목적**: 주제 소개 + 정의 + 중요성 언급

```
Global warming refers to the gradual increase in Earth's average
temperature due to human activities. It is one of the most pressing
environmental challenges of our time, affecting ecosystems, weather
patterns, and human life across the globe.
```

**핵심 요소**:
- **정의**: Global warming = gradual increase in Earth's temperature
- **원인 암시**: due to human activities
- **중요성**: most pressing environmental challenges

---

### 2. Body (본론) ~180 words

**목적**: 원인 + 영향 + 해결책 전개

#### 원인 (Causes)
| 원인 | 영어 표현 |
|------|----------|
| 화석연료 연소 | burning of fossil fuels |
| 삼림벌채 | deforestation |
| 산업화 | industrialization |
| 온실가스 배출 | greenhouse gas emissions |

#### 영향 (Effects)
| 영향 | 영어 표현 |
|------|----------|
| 빙하 녹음 | melting of glaciers |
| 해수면 상승 | rising sea levels |
| 극단적 기상 | extreme weather events |
| 생태계 파괴 | ecosystem disruption |

#### 해결책 (Solutions)
| 해결책 | 영어 표현 |
|------|----------|
| 재생에너지 사용 | use renewable energy |
| 배출량 감소 | reduce emissions |
| 나무 심기 | plant more trees |
| 인식 제고 | raise awareness |

---

### 3. Conclusion (결론) ~60 words

**목적**: 요약 + 행동 촉구

```
In conclusion, global warming poses a serious threat to our planet.
It is essential that governments, industries, and individuals work
together to reduce carbon emissions and adopt sustainable practices.
Only through collective action can we hope to mitigate the effects
of climate change for future generations.
```

**핵심 요소**:
- **요약**: poses a serious threat
- **행동 촉구**: work together, collective action
- **미래 지향**: for future generations

## 핵심 문장 패턴

### Introduction 패턴
| 패턴 | 예시 |
|------|------|
| X refers to... | Global warming **refers to** the gradual increase... |
| It is one of the most... | **It is one of the most** pressing challenges... |
| ...affecting X, Y, and Z | ...affecting ecosystems, weather patterns, **and** human life |

### Body 패턴
| 패턴 | 예시 |
|------|------|
| The main causes include... | **The main causes include** burning fossil fuels and deforestation. |
| As a result, ... | **As a result,** sea levels are rising dramatically. |
| To address this issue, ... | **To address this issue,** we must reduce emissions. |

### Conclusion 패턴
| 패턴 | 예시 |
|------|------|
| In conclusion, ... | **In conclusion,** global warming poses a serious threat. |
| It is essential that... | **It is essential that** we take immediate action. |
| Only through X can we... | **Only through** collective action **can we** mitigate... |

## 고급 어휘 & 연어

> [!tip] B2 수준 어휘로 업그레이드
> 기본 표현 → 고급 표현으로 바꾸면 점수 향상

| 기본 표현 | B2 고급 표현 |
|----------|-------------|
| stop pollution | **mitigate** environmental damage |
| make laws | **implement** policies |
| very bad results | **devastating** consequences |
| slowly remove | **phase out** fossil fuels |
| get smaller | **reduce** emissions |
