# Passive Voice (수동태)

---

## 📖 문법 설명

### 1. 핵심 개념

**수동태는 "행위를 받는 대상"이 문장의 주어가 되어, 누가 했는지보다 무슨 일이 일어났는지에 초점을 맞추는 문법 구조다.**

### 2. 왜 이 문법이 존재하는가

영어에서 수동태가 존재하는 이유는 다음과 같다:

- **행위자가 불분명하거나 중요하지 않을 때**: "My wallet was stolen." (누가 훔쳤는지 모름)
- **행위자가 너무 명백할 때**: "He was arrested." (경찰이 체포한 것이 당연)
- **행위의 결과에 집중하고 싶을 때**: "The bridge was built in 1990." (다리가 지어진 사실이 중요)
- **객관적이고 격식 있는 톤을 유지할 때**: 학술 논문, 뉴스 보도, 공식 문서에서 자주 사용
- **행위자를 일부러 숨기고 싶을 때**: "Mistakes were made." (책임 회피)

### 3. 기본 형태 & 구조

#### 기본 공식
```
주어 + be동사 + 과거분사(V3) + (by + 행위자)
```

#### 능동태 → 수동태 변환
| 능동태 | 수동태 |
|--------|--------|
| Tom **writes** a letter. | A letter **is written** by Tom. |
| Tom **wrote** a letter. | A letter **was written** by Tom. |
| Tom **will write** a letter. | A letter **will be written** by Tom. |

#### 긍정문 / 부정문 / 의문문
| 형태 | 예문 |
|------|------|
| 긍정문 | The cake **is made** by her. |
| 부정문 | The cake **isn't made** by her. |
| 의문문 | **Is** the cake **made** by her? |
| 의문사 의문문 | **When was** the cake **made**? |

#### 축약형
- is not → isn't
- are not → aren't
- was not → wasn't
- were not → weren't
- has not been → hasn't been
- have not been → haven't been
- will not be → won't be
- cannot be → can't be

### 4. 핵심 용법

#### 용법 1: 행위자가 불분명하거나 알 수 없을 때
> *My car **was stolen** last night.*
> (누가 훔쳤는지 모름)

#### 용법 2: 행위자가 명백하여 굳이 말할 필요가 없을 때
> *The suspect **was arrested** yesterday.*
> (경찰이 체포한 것이 당연)

#### 용법 3: 행위보다 결과에 초점을 맞출 때
> *The Eiffel Tower **was built** in 1889.*
> (누가 지었는지보다 언제 지어졌는지가 중요)

#### 용법 4: 객관적/격식체 문장에서
> *The data **has been analyzed** and the results **have been published**.*
> (학술 논문 스타일)

#### 용법 5: 지시/안내문에서
> *Smoking **is not allowed** in this area.*
> *All applications **must be received** before May 31.*

#### 용법 6: 뉴스/보도에서
> *Five people **were injured** in the accident.*
> *The new policy **has been announced** by the government.*

### 5. 시간 표현 / 신호 단어

수동태와 자주 쓰이는 시간 표현:

| 시간 표현 | 예문 |
|-----------|------|
| yesterday, last week/month/year | The report **was submitted** yesterday. |
| in 1990, in the 19th century | The bridge **was constructed** in 1920. |
| recently, lately | New features **have been added** recently. |
| already, just, yet | The package **has already been delivered**. |
| by + 시점 | The work **will have been completed** by Friday. |
| every day, always | Fresh bread **is baked** every morning. |

### 6. 비슷한 문법과의 비교

#### 수동태 vs 능동태: 언제 무엇을 쓸까?

| 상황 | 선택 | 이유 |
|------|------|------|
| 행위자가 중요할 때 | 능동태 | "Shakespeare wrote Hamlet." |
| 행위 결과가 중요할 때 | 수동태 | "Hamlet was written in 1600." |
| 명확하고 직접적인 톤 | 능동태 | 일상 대화, 비즈니스 이메일 |
| 격식 있고 객관적인 톤 | 수동태 | 학술 논문, 공식 보고서 |

#### be + 과거분사 vs be + 형용사

| 구조 | 의미 | 예문 |
|------|------|------|
| be + PP (수동태) | 동작을 받음 | The door **was opened** by him. (그가 열었다) |
| be + 형용사 | 상태 | The door **was open**. (열려 있는 상태) |

> **구별법**: by + 행위자를 붙일 수 있으면 수동태, 없으면 형용사

### 7. 한국인이 자주 틀리는 포인트

#### ❌ 오류 1: 자동사를 수동태로 만드는 실수

자동사(목적어를 안 갖는 동사)는 수동태로 만들 수 없다!

| 자동사 | ❌ 틀린 표현 | ✅ 올바른 표현 |
|--------|-------------|---------------|
| appear | The sun was appeared. | The sun appeared. |
| arrive | The plane was arrived. | The plane arrived. |
| happen | The accident was happened. | The accident happened. |
| occur | The problem was occurred. | The problem occurred. |
| fall | The boy was fallen. | The boy fell. |
| vanish | The ghost was vanished. | The ghost vanished. |
| exist | The problem was existed. | The problem existed. |
| die | He was died. | He died. |

#### ❌ 오류 2: 수동태 과용

한국어 "~되다/~지다" 표현 때문에 영어에서도 불필요하게 수동태를 쓰는 경향이 있다.

| ❌ 어색한 수동태 | ✅ 자연스러운 능동태 |
|-----------------|---------------------|
| It is thought by me that... | I think that... |
| The movie was watched by us. | We watched the movie. |

#### ❌ 오류 3: be동사 누락

| ❌ 틀린 표현 | ✅ 올바른 표현 |
|-------------|---------------|
| The letter written by her. | The letter **was** written by her. |
| The cake made yesterday. | The cake **was** made yesterday. |

#### ❌ 오류 4: 과거분사 대신 과거형 사용

| ❌ 틀린 표현 | ✅ 올바른 표현 |
|-------------|---------------|
| The window was broke. | The window was **broken**. |
| The food was ate. | The food was **eaten**. |

### 8. 고급 뉘앙스 & 원어민 패턴

#### 원어민이 자주 쓰는 수동태 패턴

**1. It is said/believed/thought that... (전해지기로는...)**
> *It's said that he's the richest man in town.*
> *It's believed that the company will merge.*

**2. be supposed to (하기로 되어 있다)**
> *You're supposed to be here by 9.*
> *This was supposed to be finished yesterday.*

**3. get + 과거분사 (구어체 수동태)**
> *I got fired last week.* (잘렸어)
> *She got promoted!* (승진했대!)
> *We got stuck in traffic.* (막혔어)

**4. have/get something done (사역 수동태)**
> *I had my hair cut.* (머리 잘랐어 - 미용실에서)
> *I got my car fixed.* (차 고쳤어 - 정비소에서)

**5. 비인칭 수동태 (격식체)**
> *It has been decided that the meeting will be postponed.*
> *It was announced that the CEO would resign.*

## 📚 시제별 수동태 완전 정복

### 기본 시제 (Simple Tenses)

#### 1. 현재 시제 (Present Simple)
**am/is/are + 과거분사**

| 주어 | 구조 | 예문 |
|------|------|------|
| I | am + PP | I **am called** Tom. |
| He/She/It | is + PP | The letter **is written** every day. |
| You/We/They | are + PP | The windows **are cleaned** weekly. |

#### 2. 과거 시제 (Past Simple)
**was/were + 과거분사**

| 주어 | 구조 | 예문 |
|------|------|------|
| I/He/She/It | was + PP | The cake **was made** yesterday. |
| You/We/They | were + PP | The emails **were sent** last night. |

#### 3. 미래 시제 (Future Simple)
**will be + 과거분사**

| 예문 |
|------|
| The package **will be delivered** tomorrow. |
| The results **will be announced** next week. |

---

### 진행 시제 (Continuous/Progressive Tenses)

#### 4. 현재진행 시제 (Present Continuous)
**am/is/are being + 과거분사**

| 주어 | 예문 |
|------|------|
| I | I **am being interviewed** right now. |
| He/She/It | The bridge **is being repaired** this week. |
| You/We/They | New houses **are being built** in this area. |

#### 5. 과거진행 시제 (Past Continuous)
**was/were being + 과거분사**

| 주어 | 예문 |
|------|------|
| I/He/She/It | The car **was being washed** when I arrived. |
| You/We/They | The documents **were being reviewed** at that time. |

> ⚠️ **미래진행 수동태**는 이론상 "will be being + PP"이지만, 실제로는 거의 사용되지 않는다.

---

### 완료 시제 (Perfect Tenses)

#### 6. 현재완료 시제 (Present Perfect)
**have/has been + 과거분사**

| 주어 | 구조 | 예문 |
|------|------|------|
| I/You/We/They | have been + PP | The reports **have been submitted**. |
| He/She/It | has been + PP | The window **has been broken**. |

#### 7. 과거완료 시제 (Past Perfect)
**had been + 과거분사**

| 예문 |
|------|
| The work **had been completed** before the deadline. |
| By the time I arrived, the cake **had been eaten**. |

#### 8. 미래완료 시제 (Future Perfect)
**will have been + 과거분사**

| 예문 |
|------|
| The project **will have been finished** by next month. |
| By 2030, the building **will have been demolished**. |

---

### 완료진행 시제 (Perfect Continuous Tenses)

> ⚠️ 완료진행 수동태는 **매우 드물게** 사용된다. 대부분 능동태로 표현하는 것이 자연스럽다.

#### 9. 현재완료진행 수동태 (Present Perfect Continuous Passive)
**have/has been being + 과거분사** (거의 사용 안 함)

| 이론적 구조 | 실제 사용 |
|-------------|-----------|
| The road has been being repaired for months. | The road has been under repair for months. |

#### 10. 과거완료진행 수동태 (Past Perfect Continuous Passive)
**had been being + 과거분사** (거의 사용 안 함)

#### 11. 미래완료진행 수동태 (Future Perfect Continuous Passive)
**will have been being + 과거분사** (사실상 사용 안 함)

> 💡 **실전 팁**: 완료진행 수동태가 필요한 상황에서는 다른 표현으로 대체하자!
> - ❌ The house has been being built for two years.
> - ✅ The house has been under construction for two years.
> - ✅ They have been building the house for two years. (능동태)

---

### 조동사 + 수동태 (Modal Verbs + Passive)

**조동사 + be + 과거분사**

| 조동사 | 구조 | 예문 | 의미 |
|--------|------|------|------|
| can | can be + PP | This **can be done** easily. | 가능 |
| could | could be + PP | It **could be finished** by tomorrow. | 가능성 |
| may | may be + PP | The meeting **may be postponed**. | 추측/허락 |
| might | might be + PP | She **might be invited** to the party. | 낮은 가능성 |
| must | must be + PP | The report **must be submitted** today. | 의무/강한 추측 |
| should | should be + PP | Children **should be protected**. | 당위 |
| ought to | ought to be + PP | This **ought to be fixed** soon. | 당위 |
| have to | have to be + PP | It **has to be done** now. | 필수 |
| need to | need to be + PP | The car **needs to be repaired**. | 필요 |
| used to | used to be + PP | This building **used to be called** "City Hall." | 과거 습관 |

#### 조동사 수동태의 부정문과 의문문

| 형태 | 예문 |
|------|------|
| 부정문 | It **can't be done**. / It **shouldn't be ignored**. |
| 의문문 | **Can** it **be fixed**? / **Should** the rules **be changed**? |

#### 조동사 완료 수동태 (과거 추측/유감)

**조동사 + have been + 과거분사**

| 예문 | 의미 |
|------|------|
| It **must have been stolen**. | 틀림없이 도난당했을 것이다 |
| It **could have been prevented**. | 예방될 수 있었을 텐데 |
| It **should have been done** earlier. | 더 일찍 되었어야 했다 |
| It **might have been lost**. | 잃어버렸을 수도 있다 |

---

### to 부정사 + 수동태 (Infinitive Passive)

**to be + 과거분사**

| 용법 | 예문 |
|------|------|
| want to be + PP | I **want to be loved**. |
| need to be + PP | This **needs to be fixed**. |
| expect to be + PP | I **expect to be promoted** soon. |
| seem to be + PP | He **seems to be confused**. |
| appear to be + PP | The problem **appears to be solved**. |
| deserve to be + PP | She **deserves to be recognized**. |
| be likely to be + PP | The event **is likely to be cancelled**. |

#### 완료 부정사 수동태 (과거 사실)

**to have been + 과거분사**

| 예문 | 의미 |
|------|------|
| He seems **to have been injured**. | 다쳤던 것 같다 |
| The document appears **to have been forged**. | 위조된 것 같다 |
| She claims **to have been cheated**. | 사기당했다고 주장한다 |

## 🔑 by 생략 규칙 (When to Omit the Agent)

### 언제 "by + 행위자"를 생략할까?

수동태 문장의 약 80%는 **by + 행위자 없이** 사용된다!

#### 1. 행위자가 불분명하거나 알 수 없을 때 ✅ 생략
> *My wallet **was stolen**.* (누가 훔쳤는지 모름)
> *The window **has been broken**.* (누가 깼는지 불명)

#### 2. 행위자가 일반적인 사람들일 때 ✅ 생략
someone, they, people, one 등이 행위자일 때

| 능동태 | 수동태 (by 생략) |
|--------|-----------------|
| Someone stole my bike. | My bike **was stolen**. |
| They speak English here. | English **is spoken** here. |
| People recycle plastic. | Plastic **is recycled**. |

#### 3. 행위자가 너무 명백할 때 ✅ 생략

| 상황 | 예문 | 생략되는 행위자 |
|------|------|----------------|
| 체포/기소 | He **was arrested** yesterday. | by the police |
| 수술/치료 | She **was operated on** last week. | by the doctor |
| 교육 | Children **are taught** to read at school. | by teachers |
| 법적 절차 | The case **will be tried** next month. | by the court |

#### 4. 행위자를 언급하고 싶지 않을 때 ✅ 생략
> *Mistakes **were made**.* (책임 회피)
> *You **will be notified** soon.* (누가 알려줄지 중요하지 않음)

#### 5. 공식적/객관적 톤을 유지할 때 ✅ 생략
> *The experiment **was conducted** under controlled conditions.*
> *It **has been proven** that exercise improves health.*

---

### 언제 "by + 행위자"를 반드시 써야 할까?

#### 1. 행위자가 새롭고 중요한 정보일 때 ✅ 포함
> *This novel **was written by** a 12-year-old girl.* (놀라운 정보)
> *The painting **was sold by** Christie's for $5 million.* (구체적 정보)

#### 2. 유명인/특정인이 행위자일 때 ✅ 포함
> *Hamlet **was written by** Shakespeare.*
> *The iPhone **was invented by** Steve Jobs.*
> *This photo **was taken by** my grandmother.*

#### 3. 대조를 표현할 때 ✅ 포함
> *The book was praised **by critics** but criticized **by readers**.*

---

### by 외의 전치사

| 전치사 | 상황 | 예문 |
|--------|------|------|
| **with** | 도구/수단 | The door was opened **with** a key. |
| **by** | 행위자 | The door was opened **by** the guard. |
| **in** | 감정의 원인 | I was disappointed **in** the result. |
| **at** | 감정의 대상 | She was amazed **at** his talent. |
| **about** | 감정의 주제 | We're excited **about** the news. |

## 🔍 오류 교정 (Error Correction)

### 초급 (Beginner)
1. ❌ The accident was happened yesterday on the highway.
> [!success]- 정답 및 해설
> ✅ The accident **happened** yesterday on the highway.
> **해설**: happen은 자동사로, 수동태로 만들 수 없다. was happened → happened

2. ❌ The movie was watched by we last night.
> [!success]- 정답 및 해설
> ✅ The movie was watched by **us** last night.
> **해설**: 전치사 by 뒤에는 목적격(us)이 와야 한다. we → us

3. ❌ The window was broke by the ball.
> [!success]- 정답 및 해설
> ✅ The window was **broken** by the ball.
> **해설**: 수동태에서는 과거분사(broken)를 써야 한다. broke(과거형) → broken(과거분사)

4. ❌ The problem was occured because of the system error.
> [!success]- 정답 및 해설
> ✅ The problem **occurred** because of the system error.
> **해설**: occur는 자동사로, 수동태로 만들 수 없다. was occured → occurred

### 중급 (Intermediate)
5. ❌ The report must completed by Friday.
> [!success]- 정답 및 해설
> ✅ The report must **be** completed by Friday.
> **해설**: 조동사 수동태는 "조동사 + be + PP" 형태여야 한다. be 누락

6. ❌ The meeting has cancelled due to the storm.
> [!success]- 정답 및 해설
> ✅ The meeting has **been** cancelled due to the storm.
> **해설**: 현재완료 수동태는 "have/has + been + PP" 형태여야 한다. been 누락

7. ❌ The new products are being advertised by the company's marketing team since last month.
> [!success]- 정답 및 해설
> ✅ The new products **have been being advertised** (또는 **have been advertised**) by the company's marketing team since last month.
> **해설**: "since last month"은 현재완료(진행)와 함께 쓰인다. are being → have been

8. ❌ The criminal was escaped from the prison last night.
> [!success]- 정답 및 해설
> ✅ The criminal **escaped** from the prison last night.
> **해설**: escape는 자동사로, 수동태로 만들 수 없다.

### 고급 (Advanced)
9. ❌ The artifact is believed to made by ancient craftsmen over 3,000 years ago.
> [!success]- 정답 및 해설
> ✅ The artifact is believed to **have been** made by ancient craftsmen over 3,000 years ago.
> **해설**: 과거 사실에 대한 믿음을 표현할 때는 완료 부정사 수동태(to have been + PP)를 사용한다.

10. ❌ Had the safety protocols being followed, the disaster could have been avoided.
> [!success]- 정답 및 해설
> ✅ Had the safety protocols **been** followed, the disaster could have been avoided.
> **해설**: 가정법 과거완료 도치 구문에서는 "Had + S + been + PP" 형태를 사용한다. being → been

> [!success]- 정답 및 해설
> ✅ The investigation results are expected to **be announced** next week.
> **해설**: "results"가 발표하는 것이 아니라 발표되는 것이므로 수동태가 필요하다.

12. ❌ The new regulation, having approved by the committee, will take effect next month.
> [!success]- 정답 및 해설
> ✅ The new regulation, having **been** approved by the committee, will take effect next month.
> **해설**: 분사구문의 완료 수동태는 "having been + PP" 형태여야 한다. been 누락

> - "is estimated to have been made" → "is thought to have been created"

15. **위원회에 의해 승인된 후, 그 제안은 수정 없이 시행되어야 하며, 모든 관련 부서에 통보되어야 한다.**
> [!success]- 모범 답안
> **Having been approved** by the committee, the proposal **must be implemented** without modification and all relevant departments **must be notified**.
>
> **격차 체크포인트:**
> - Having been + PP (완료 분사구문 수동태)
> - must be + PP (의무 수동태)
>
> **대안 표현:**
> - "Having been approved" → "Once approved" / "After being approved"

16. **이 연구 결과는 여러 독립적인 팀에 의해 검증되었으며, 결과가 재현될 수 있다면 곧 학술지에 게재될 것으로 예상된다.**
> [!success]- 모범 답안
> These research findings **have been verified** by several independent teams, and **if the results can be replicated**, they **are expected to be published** in academic journals soon.
>
> **격차 체크포인트:**
> - have been + PP (현재완료 수동태)
> - can be + PP (가능 수동태)
> - are expected to be + PP (기대 수동태)
>
> **대안 표현:**
> - "can be replicated" → "are able to be replicated"

17. **범인은 아직 체포되지 않았지만, 용의자로 여겨지는 사람이 이미 확인되었고, 그의 행방이 추적되고 있다.**
> [!success]- 모범 답안
> The perpetrator **has not yet been arrested**, but a person **believed to be** the suspect **has already been identified**, and his whereabouts **are being tracked**.
>
> **격차 체크포인트:**
> - has not been + PP (현재완료 수동태 부정)
> - believed to be (형용사적 분사구문)
> - are being + PP (현재진행 수동태)
>
> **대안 표현:**
> - "believed to be the suspect" → "suspected to be the perpetrator"

18. **회사의 기밀 정보가 해킹 공격으로 유출되었을 가능성이 있으며, 만약 그렇다면 모든 비밀번호가 즉시 변경되어야 한다.**
> [!success]- 모범 답안
> The company's confidential information **may have been leaked** through a hacking attack, and if so, all passwords **must be changed** immediately.
>
> **격차 체크포인트:**
> - may have been + PP (추측 + 완료 수동태)
> - must be + PP (의무 수동태)
>
> **대안 표현:**
> - "may have been leaked" → "might have been compromised"

19. **환자는 수술 전에 금식하도록 지시받았고, 수술 후에는 최소 24시간 동안 모니터링 될 것이며, 필요하다면 추가 검사가 실시될 것이다.**
> [!success]- 모범 답안
> The patient **was instructed to fast** before surgery, **will be monitored** for at least 24 hours after surgery, and additional tests **will be conducted** if necessary.
>
> **격차 체크포인트:**
> - was instructed to (tell 유형 동사의 수동태 + to 부정사)
> - will be + PP (미래 수동태)
>
> **대안 표현:**
> - "was instructed to fast" → "was told to fast" / "was advised to fast"

20. **그 다리는 100년 전에 지어졌고, 여러 번 보수되어 왔으며, 앞으로 50년 동안은 더 이상의 대규모 수리 없이 사용될 수 있을 것으로 추정된다.**
> [!success]- 모범 답안
> The bridge **was built** 100 years ago, **has been renovated** multiple times, and **is estimated to be usable** for the next 50 years without any major repairs.
>
> **격차 체크포인트:**
> - was + PP (과거 수동태)
> - has been + PP (현재완료 수동태)
> - is estimated to be + 형용사
>
> **대안 표현:**
> - "is estimated to be usable" → "is expected to last" / "is projected to remain functional"

21. **모든 증거가 제출된 후에야 최종 판결이 내려질 것이며, 그 결정은 항소될 수 있다.**
> [!success]- 모범 답안
> The final verdict **will not be delivered until** all evidence **has been submitted**, and the decision **can be appealed**.
>
> **격차 체크포인트:**
> - will not be + PP + until (~ 전에는 ~되지 않을 것)
> - has been + PP (현재완료 수동태, 시간 부사절)
> - can be + PP (가능 수동태)
>
> **대안 표현:**
> - "will not be delivered until" → "will only be delivered after"

22. **해당 법률이 개정되지 않는 한, 이러한 관행은 계속 허용될 것이며, 더 많은 피해자가 발생할 수 있다는 우려가 제기되어 왔다.**
> [!success]- 모범 답안
> **Unless the law is amended**, such practices **will continue to be permitted**, and concerns **have been raised** that more victims may emerge.
>
> **격차 체크포인트:**
> - Unless + S + is + PP (조건 부사절 수동태)
> - will continue to be + PP (계속 수동태)
> - have been raised (제기되다)
>
> **대안 표현:**
> - "Unless the law is amended" → "If the law is not revised"

23. **그 원고는 편집자에 의해 여러 차례 수정되도록 요청받았으며, 최종본이 승인되면 내년 초에 출판될 예정이다.**
> [!success]- 모범 답안
> The manuscript **was requested to be revised** multiple times by the editor, and once the final version **is approved**, it **is scheduled to be published** early next year.
>
> **격차 체크포인트:**
> - was requested to be + PP (요청 동사 수동태 + to 부정사)
> - is approved (조건절 현재 시제)
> - is scheduled to be + PP (예정 수동태)
>
> **대안 표현:**
> - "was requested to be revised" → "was asked to be modified"

24. **신기술이 도입되면, 현재 수동으로 수행되고 있는 많은 작업들이 자동화될 것이며, 결과적으로 일부 직책은 제거될 수 있다.**
> [!success]- 모범 답안
> Once new technology **is introduced**, many tasks that **are currently being performed** manually **will be automated**, and as a result, some positions **may be eliminated**.
>
> **격차 체크포인트:**
> - is introduced (조건/시간절 현재 시제)
> - are being + PP (현재진행 수동태)
> - will be + PP (미래 수동태)
> - may be + PP (가능성 수동태)
>
> **대안 표현:**
> - "may be eliminated" → "could be removed" / "might be cut"

25. **그 예술 작품은 원래 가치의 두 배에 낙찰되었고, 전문가들에 의해 진품으로 감정되었으며, 현재 개인 소장가에 의해 소장되어 있다.**
> [!success]- 모범 답안
> The artwork **was sold at auction** for twice its original value, **was authenticated as genuine** by experts, and **is currently being kept** by a private collector.
>
> **격차 체크포인트:**
> - was sold at auction (경매에서 팔리다)
> - was authenticated as (~ 로 감정되다)
> - is being kept (현재진행 수동태)
>
> **대안 표현:**
> - "is currently being kept" → "is now owned by" / "is currently housed with"

> - 누구에 의해 감독되는지
> - 언제까지 완료되어야 하는지
> - 결과물이 어떻게 평가될 것인지

## 📝 학습 정리

### 핵심 공식
```
수동태 = 주어 + be동사 + 과거분사(V3) + (by 행위자)
```

### 시제별 be동사
| 시제 | be동사 형태 |
|------|-------------|
| 현재 | am/is/are |
| 과거 | was/were |
| 미래 | will be |
| 현재진행 | am/is/are being |
| 과거진행 | was/were being |
| 현재완료 | have/has been |
| 과거완료 | had been |
| 미래완료 | will have been |

### 꼭 기억할 것
1. **자동사는 수동태 불가**: happen, occur, appear, arrive, fall, vanish, die, exist
2. **by 생략**: 행위자가 불분명/명백/일반적일 때
3. **get 수동태**: 구어체에서 자주 사용 (get fired, get promoted)
4. **완료 부정사 수동태**: to have been + PP (과거 사실)
