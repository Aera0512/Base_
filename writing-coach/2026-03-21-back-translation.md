# Back-Translation Session: Digital Focus

## Model Text (English Original)

In an age dominated by digital devices, the ability to maintain deep focus has become increasingly rare. While technology offers unprecedented access to information, it simultaneously fragments our attention through constant notifications and endless scrolling. Research suggests that the average person checks their smartphone over eighty times a day, which inevitably disrupts sustained concentration. This phenomenon is particularly concerning for students and professionals whose work demands prolonged mental engagement. Rather than passively accepting this reality, we must actively cultivate habits that protect our attention. Simple strategies such as designated phone-free periods and mindful technology use can significantly restore our capacity for deep work. Ultimately, those who master the art of focus in this distracted world will possess a considerable advantage.
