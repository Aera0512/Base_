# Back-Translation Session: Social Media and Mental Health

## Model Text (English Original)

The pervasive influence of social media on mental health has become an increasingly urgent topic of discussion among researchers. While these platforms were designed to foster connection, excessive use can be detrimental to well-being, particularly among adolescents. Social comparison, fueled by curated images, often erodes self-esteem and triggers anxiety in vulnerable users. Each notification delivers a small dopamine hit, reinforcing an addictive cycle that is difficult to break. As a consequence, cyberbullying and body image issues have exacerbated the problem further. Nevertheless, social media also provides genuine benefits to some extent. In light of this complexity, the most effective approach is to mitigate harmful effects through conscious habits such as a regular digital detox, rather than abandoning these platforms altogether.
