## 🎯 The CMDS Process

```
┌──────────┐     ┌────────┐     ┌─────────┐     ┌───────┐
│ Connect  │ ──> │ Merge  │ ──> │ Develop │ ──> │ Share │
│   100    │     │  200   │     │ 300-600 │     │700-800│
└──────────┘     └────────┘     └─────────┘     └───────┘
    ↓                ↓               ↓               ↓
  Themes        Literature    Data/Methods     Outputs
```

### How It Works

1. **Connect (100)** - Capture interesting ideas and themes
2. **Merge (200)** - Study and synthesize existing knowledge  
3. **Develop (300-600)** - Apply data, methods, tools, build expertise
4. **Share (700-800)** - Create content and deliver outputs

## 💡 Tips for Success

### Start Small
- Begin with [[나머지/도구와 자동화/CMDS/00. Inbox/README|Inbox]] → capture everything
- Process regularly (daily/weekly)
- Don't worry about perfect categorization at first

### Build Connections
- Use `[[wikilinks]]` liberally
- Link forward and backward
- Let your knowledge graph grow organically

### Review Regularly
- Daily: Process inbox
- Weekly: Review and refine
- Monthly: Assess system and adjust

### Evolve Your System
- The CMDS framework is flexible
- Customize subcategories as needed
- Add what works, remove what doesn't
