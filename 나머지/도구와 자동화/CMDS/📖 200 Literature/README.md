**The Knowledge Layer** - Concepts, frameworks, theories, classics, reviews

## Purpose

This is your theoretical foundation - where you synthesize knowledge from books, papers, and other sources.

## What Goes Here

- Book notes and reviews
- Framework summaries (e.g., "Getting Things Done", "Zettelkasten")
- Theory explanations
- Literature reviews
- Concept syntheses
- Classic works analysis

## CMDS Process Phase

**Merge (통합)** - Literature and knowledge integration

This is the second phase where you:
- Review existing frameworks and theories
- Synthesize concepts from multiple sources
- Create literature notes and summaries
- Build your theoretical foundation

## Subcategories

Create subcategories (📚 201-299) for different domains:
- 📚 210 PKM & Knowledge Management
- 📚 220 Productivity & GTD
- 📚 230 Learning & Education
- 📚 240 Technology & AI
- etc.

## Workflow

1. **Read/Consume** - Books, papers, articles
2. **Capture** - Key insights in `20. Literature Notes/`
3. **Synthesize** - Create summary here
4. **Connect** - Link to related themes (100) and permanent notes (30)
5. **Apply** - Use in outputs (800)

## Examples

- `Getting Things Done - David Allen` - Productivity framework
- `Zettelkasten Method - Niklas Luhmann` - Note-taking system
- `Atomic Habits - James Clear` - Behavior change framework
- `Second Brain - Tiago Forte` - Digital organization system
- `Deep Work - Cal Newport` - Focus methodology

## Tips

- Write in your own words
- Extract actionable insights
- Link to source material
- Connect to personal experience
- Update as understanding deepens
