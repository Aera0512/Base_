# 📖 900 Divisions

허브: [[나머지/도구와 자동화/CMDS/🏠 Vault Home]] · [[나머지/도구와 자동화/CMDS/📖 900 Divisions/Hub - 📖 900 Divisions]]


**The Operations Layer** - 9 operational divisions

## Purpose

This is your organizational structure for managing different areas of work and life.

## What Goes Here

- Work divisions and departments
- Personal project areas
- Team structures
- Operational workflows
- Business units
- Life areas and domains
- Organizational systems
- Management structures

## CMDS Process Phase

**Share (공유)** - Operations and management

This supports the sharing phase by:
- Organizing work structures
- Managing teams and projects
- Coordinating operations
- Maintaining systems

## Subcategories

Create subcategories (📚 901-999) for different divisions:
- 📚 910 Academic/Research
- 📚 920 Teaching/Education
- 📚 930 Consulting
- 📚 940 Content Creation
- 📚 950 Personal Development
- 📚 960 Family & Relationships
- etc.

Customize based on your actual operational structure.

## Workflow

1. **Define** - Identify operational divisions
2. **Organize** - Structure each division
3. **Manage** - Track division activities
4. **Coordinate** - Integrate across divisions
5. **Review** - Assess division performance

## Examples

- `Academic Research Division - Structure` - Work area
- `Online Course Business - Operations` - Business unit
- `Family Management System` - Life area
- `Consulting Practice - Client Management` - Professional division
- `Content Creation Pipeline` - Creative operation
- `Personal Growth Projects` - Development area

## Tips

- Align with real-life structure
- Review and adjust regularly
- Link to relevant outputs (800)
- Coordinate with creative work (700)
- Track projects and initiatives
- Maintain clear boundaries
- Document processes
