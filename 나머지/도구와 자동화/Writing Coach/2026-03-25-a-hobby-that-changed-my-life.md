## Model Text (원문)
When I first embarked on learning to play the piano three years ago, I had no idea how profoundly it would reshape my life. What began as an idle curiosity quickly evolved into a genuine passion that demanded both discipline and dedication. Over time, the daily practice sessions became therapeutic — an escape from the pressures of work and a space where I could focus entirely on the present moment. What struck me most was how the process of mastering a difficult piece cultivated resilience; each failure taught me to approach problems with a different mindset rather than giving up. More importantly, this hobby broadened my perspective on what personal fulfillment truly means. It was not until I performed in front of an audience for the first time that I understood the transformative power of stepping outside one's comfort zone. In hindsight, picking up the piano was not merely about learning an instrument — it was about discovering a better version of myself.
